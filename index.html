Posterior vs anterior — what changes

The sample form uses almost the same Palpation / Percussion / Auscultation wording for both sides. Inspection is different.

Posterior inspection extra / specific: spinous processes in a straight line; elliptical thorax with ribs sloping ~45° to the spine; scapulae symmetric in each hemithorax.

Anterior inspection extra / specific: normal elliptical chest wall; ribs sloping downward with symmetric interspaces; costal angle within 90°; relaxed facial expression without nasal flaring.

Shared inspection: skin colour consistent with genetic background; nails normal configuration; RR 14/min regular even; relaxed respiration without accessory muscle; no audible breathing sound.

Keyword checklist (posterior)

If you can hit these keywords, the marker usually accepts the finding.

spinous processes straight thorax symmetric elliptical ribs 45° to spine scapulae symmetric skin colour nails normal RR 14 regular even no accessory muscle no audible sound expansion symmetric tactile fremitus equal skin warm no mass/lesion/tenderness equal resonance air entry equal breath sounds clear no adventitious sound

Mini mnemonic

I-P-P-A = Inspect → Palpate → Percuss → Auscultate (never skip order).

Posterior inspect: Spine – Shape 45° – Scapulae – Skin – Nails – 14 – Relaxed – Silent

Palpate: Expand – Fremitus – Skin

Percuss: Resonance both sides
